Operating rhythm Plan

  • Daily Finance check-in on Teams and a brief end-of-day exception note.
  • Weekly Finance team meeting, Aspiro internal quality review and a 30-minute CEO check-in.
  • Month-end close, then the CEO Finance report by working day 10.
  • Board and DAB papers to their calendars, reviewed by William before issue.
  • Escalation the same day: Finance team to Ashish, Ashish to William, William to the CEO.
  • One AIB-hosted Finance operating register holds priorities, risks, decisions and aged items.

What changes at handover

  • The CFO keeps the register, close calendar, decisions log, desk notes, KPI definitions and data feeds.
  • Aspiro's value scorecard and value log close out as the engagement record.
  • William's independent review of Board and DAB packs becomes the CFO's own sign-off route.
  • The delegated authority below is replaced by the permanent CFO's DAB-approved authority.
  • At least two weeks of overlap; the interim lead stays reachable during the first close.
  • AIB leads the CFO search and the DAB approval; handover follows both.

Delegated authority during the interim Plan

Aspiro leads Finance as a service within the CEO's written delegation. No Aspiro individual is appointed CFO or joins the Board of Management, and Aspiro is never a payment signatory.

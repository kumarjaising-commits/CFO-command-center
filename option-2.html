Executive Governance & Variance Commentary

Board-level analysis generated for the Board Audit & Risk Committee

1. Revenue & Yield Dynamics

Net interest income remains the principal growth engine ($27.05M YTD), expanding +4.8% over budget. Higher placement rates on central bank deposits compensated for a minor softening in fee income (-6.0%), which stemmed from trade finance delays in foreign currency transactions.

2. Cost Control & Technology Overruns

Total operating expenses reached $28.22M, staying within aggregate budget limits due to personnel savings (+$390K). However, IT expenses ran -$540K over budget due to unplanned core banking maintenance and SWIFT gateway security upgrades required for DAB compliance.

3. Balance Sheet & Asset Quality

Total assets expanded +5.4% YTD to $782.4M. Customer deposits grew +5.5% to $612.1M, maintaining a strong 78.2% deposit-to-asset ratio. Non-performing loan (NPL) coverage ratio stands at 148%, well above the regulatory requirement of 100%.
